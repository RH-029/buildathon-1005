# TravelMind illustration

`travel-postcard.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this UI.
It is decorative travel inspiration, not a photo of a recommended destination.

Final prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story
> Asset type: decorative hero illustration for the TravelMind travel recommendation website, landscape 3:2 composition.
> Primary request: A colorful, warm travel postcard illustration with a coastal town, a turquoise bay, terracotta rooftops, leafy palms, rolling lavender mountains and a small winding path. Small playful travel details like a suitcase and a paper airplane fit naturally into the scene.
> Style/medium: polished editorial illustration with flat painted shapes and subtle paper grain, modern travel magazine aesthetic.
> Color palette: coral orange, turquoise, lavender, sunny yellow, warm cream.
> Composition/framing: landscape scene with breathing room around the edges, readable at 400px wide.
> Lighting/mood: sunny, optimistic, inviting.
> Constraints: image only, no interface, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
